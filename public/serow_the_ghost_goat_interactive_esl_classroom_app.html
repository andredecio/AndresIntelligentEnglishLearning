Stage 3 of 6 • Grammar Focus

Present Simple: Facts & Daily Habits

Nature documentaries use the Present Simple for permanent facts, physical traits, and repeated animal habits.

Interactive Grammar Rule

Compare Singular vs. Plural Subjects

SINGULAR SUBJECT (1 Animal) verb + -s / -es

A serow / It / She / He...

  • • A serow lives high in mountain forests.
  • • It looks like a cross between a goat and a boar.
  • • A quick rub spreads the serow's scent.
PLURAL SUBJECT (2+ Animals) base verb (no -s)

Serows / They / Humans...

  • • Serows stand about 3 feet tall at the shoulder.
  • • At night, they sleep in hidden mountain caves.
  • • Humans pose the greatest threat to habitats.
